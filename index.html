🌟 Privacy Policy

MindFuel - Your Daily Motivation Companion

Last Updated: October 18, 2025
🔒 Our Privacy Promise:

MindFuel is a 100% OFFLINE app that collects ZERO user data. All your favorites and settings stay on your device. No tracking. No analytics. No data transmission.

📱 Information We Collect

MindFuel is designed with your privacy as the top priority. We collect NO personal information. Here's what stays on your device:

  • Your favorite quotes (stored locally)
  • App preferences (haptic feedback, notification settings)
  • Notification time preference

Important: All this data remains on your device and is never transmitted to any server or third party.

🚫 What We DON'T Collect

  • ❌ Name, email, or personal identifiable information
  • ❌ Location data
  • ❌ Usage analytics or tracking data
  • ❌ Device information
  • ❌ Contacts, photos, or files
  • ❌ Browsing history
  • ❌ Crash reports or diagnostics
  • ❌ Any other user data

🔐 How We Use Your Information

The information stored locally on your device is used solely to:

  • Remember your favorite quotes
  • Maintain your app preferences (haptic feedback, notifications)
  • Provide a personalized experience within the app

All data remains on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

📊 Data Storage and Security

Your privacy and data security are our top priorities:

  • All data is stored locally using Android's secure storage mechanisms (SharedPreferences and DataStore)
  • No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties
  • No internet connection is required for any app functionality
  • You can clear all app data through your device settings at any time
  • Notifications are scheduled locally using Android WorkManager (Google's recommended background task API)

🔌 Third-Party Services

MindFuel does NOT integrate with any third-party services:

  • ❌ No Google Analytics or similar tracking tools
  • ❌ No advertisement networks or tracking pixels
  • ❌ No social media integrations that share your data
  • ❌ No crash reporting or analytics SDKs

The sharing feature only uses your device's built-in sharing capabilities and doesn't transmit data to us.

🔑 Permissions Explained

MindFuel requests minimal permissions, all of which are optional:

POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+)

  • Purpose: Send you daily motivational quotes at your chosen time
  • Optional: Yes, can be disabled in settings
  • Data Access: None - only displays pre-loaded quotes

VIBRATE

  • Purpose: Provide haptic feedback for better user experience
  • Optional: Yes, can be disabled in app settings
  • Data Access: None - only device vibration

WAKE_LOCK & RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED

  • Purpose: Ensure notifications continue working after device restart and while device is sleeping
  • Optional: Automatic, tied to notification feature
  • Data Access: None - only for system wake-up and boot detection

👶 Children's Privacy

MindFuel is suitable for all ages:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13
  • The app contains only positive, motivational content suitable for all ages
  • No user accounts or personal information collection
  • Parents can safely allow children to use this app

🗑️ Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control:

  • Go to: Settings → Apps → MindFuel → Clear Data
  • Or simply uninstall the app
  • This will remove all locally stored data from your device

  • Any changes will be reflected in app updates
  • We will update the "Last Updated" date
  • Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of policy changes
  • We will always maintain our commitment to user privacy
